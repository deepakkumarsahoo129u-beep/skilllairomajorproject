📌 Project Overview

In today's competitive retail landscape, organizations require structured, data-driven insights to make strategic decisions. This Major Project serves as an end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and business intelligence dashboard using the Global Superstore Sales dataset. By cleaning, aggregating, and visualizing transactional data, this project uncovers actionable insights related to revenue generation, regional performance, and the financial impact of discounting strategies.

❓ Problem Statement

Retail chains often struggle to identify the root causes of profit loss despite high sales volumes. The challenge is to analyze historical sales data to determine exactly which product categories and geographic regions are driving profitability, and where the company is losing money due to operational inefficiencies or excessive discounting.

🎯 Objectives

Data Cleaning & Preparation: Transform raw transactional data into a structured format suitable for analysis by handling missing values and datetime conversions.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Group and aggregate data to uncover trends in sales and profitability across different dimensions (time, geography, category).

Diagnostic Analytics: Investigate the relationship between sales volume, discount rates, and final profit margins.

Data Storytelling & Visualization: Design a professional suite of visualizations (dashboards/charts) that clearly communicate business insights to non-technical stakeholders.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used

Python: The core programming language used for the data science workflow.

Pandas & NumPy: Utilized for data ingestion, cleaning, manipulation, and statistical aggregations.

Matplotlib & Seaborn: Used to design and generate high-quality, professional data visualizations (Heatmaps, Scatter Plots, Bar Charts).

Jupyter Notebook: The interactive development environment used to write and document the analysis.
